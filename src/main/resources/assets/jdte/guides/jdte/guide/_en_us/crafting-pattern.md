# Crafting Patterns and Essence Conversion

Right-click a Crafting Pattern in either hand to open its 3x3 ghost crafting grid. Click with an ingredient or Shift-click an inventory item to record one ingredient without consuming it. The result is a preview, not an obtainable item. Save records only a valid, ordinary crafting recipe; Clear removes the saved recipe.

Combine the recorded pattern with an Essence Conversion Upgrade in an anvil. Each binding costs one experience level and consumes one pattern. The basic upgrade accepts one pattern; the advanced upgrade accepts three distinct patterns. Only one conversion upgrade can be installed in a Greenhouse or Large Greenhouse.

Installed patterns batch-craft exact recorded ingredients from greenhouse output, including recipe remainders. Patterns sharing inputs take turns to balance their products. Insufficient ingredients stay in the machine until a complete craft is possible. A full output inventory prevents conversion without consuming its inputs. Recipes are revalidated against the current server recipes; invalid patterns are shown in tooltips and Jade.

Without recorded patterns, conversion uses unambiguous Mystical Agriculture essence-only recipes. Conversion runs before adjacent output and AE Output. The advanced upgrade is a smithing upgrade of the basic card and retains its recorded pattern.
