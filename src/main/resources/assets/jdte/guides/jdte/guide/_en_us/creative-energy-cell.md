# Creative Energy Cell

Take the cell from the creative inventory and place it next to a machine that uses FE. The cell powers adjacent devices on all six sides every tick. Cables can draw unlimited FE from any face. Use a rechargeable item on the cell to charge it.

The cell can make repeated transfers in one tick. Devices that enforce their own per-tick intake limit continue charging on subsequent ticks.
