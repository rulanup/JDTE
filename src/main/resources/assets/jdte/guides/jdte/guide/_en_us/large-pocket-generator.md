# Large Pocket Generator

<ItemImage id="jdte:large_pocket_generator" scale="2" />

The Large Pocket Generator extends JDT's pocket generator with **4x** internal FE capacity while keeping its fuel slot, toggle state, and generation behavior.

## Features

- Equips in the dedicated Curios `large_pocket_generator` slot.
- Opens through its dedicated hotkey.
- Continues charging compatible items in both inventory and Curios using JDT's existing charging behavior.
- Uses the resolved large fuel canister burn multiplier when one is inserted as fuel.
