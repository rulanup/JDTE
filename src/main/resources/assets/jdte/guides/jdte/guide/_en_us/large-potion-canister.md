# Large Potion Canister

<ItemImage id="jdte:large_potion_canister" scale="2" />

The Large Potion Canister is an expanded JDT potion canister with **4,000 mB** of storage.

## Batch filling rules

- Each fill requires **4 matching potions**.
- A successful batch adds **1,000 mB**.
- Three potions or mixed potion contents do not consume the input.
