# Extended Energy Transmitter

Use an Extended Upgrade on the native JDT Energy Transmitter to obtain eight upgrade slots. The Advanced Energy Transmitter is a separate machine, not the input to this conversion.

The Extended tier retains JDT's configured energy capacity, range, transfer limit, distance loss, filters, redstone control and particle toggle. Energy access is on the facing side. Energy Overload removes the transfer-rate limit; energy availability and receiver capacity still limit delivery. Ultimate Capacity applies its real buffer multiplier; Ultimate Overclock executes ten machine ticks per server tick.

Conversion moves only the machine's owned energy buffer. Energy in other transmitters remains with those machines. Wrench transport preserves the machine inventory, upgrades, filter and configuration in the carried block.
