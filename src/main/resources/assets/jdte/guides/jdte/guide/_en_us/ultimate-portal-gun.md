# Ultimate Portal Gun

<ItemImage id="jdte:ultimate_portal_gun" scale="2" />

The Ultimate Portal Gun retains the projectiles, FE cost, and normal destination rules of JDT's Advanced Portal Gun while expanding its fluid storage and destination management for long-term use.

## Tank and costs

The gun has one **1000 B (1,000,000 mB) Portal Fluid tank**. Right-click a Portal Fluid source to collect it. Firing also consumes the FE configured for JDT's Advanced Portal Gun. No projectile is created when either resource is insufficient.

Destinations captured directly from the world continue to use JDT's distance and cross-dimensional pricing. Manually edited coordinate slots use fixed rules:

- Same dimension: 1 B per block, capped at 500 B per shot.
- Different dimension: a fixed 1000 B, requiring a full tank.

## Destination menu

Hold the gun and press **V** to open its paged radial menu. Each page contains 12 slots, and more pages can be added, so there is no fixed destination limit.

The menu can select a destination, save the current position, clear a slot, or manually enter X/Y/Z coordinates and choose from the server's currently available dimensions. Normal left/right-click and sneaking portal controls remain consistent with the JDT portal gun.
