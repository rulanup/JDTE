# Large Fuel Canister

<ItemImage id="jdte:large_fuel_canister" scale="2" />

Stores four times the normal JDT canister's fuel: **40,000,000** fuel with default settings. Right-click while held in your main hand to open it, then insert solid furnace fuel. Fuel with a container remainder and other canisters cannot be used to fill it. Fuel that does not fit remains in the input slot and returns when you close the menu.

With Curios installed, equip it in its dedicated slot and press **F** (configurable) to open the worn canister.

Each generator fuel unit costs ten times the normal canister unit. Filling records the fuel's weighted burn speed with the source's ×10 scale; generators apply the additional ×10 large-canister speed multiplier. The normal and Extended solid JDT generators support it. Generator Upgrade needs **two complete units** for **three times** the native fuel FE credit. A full FE buffer preserves purchased generation credit until room becomes available.

## Crafting

<RecipeFor id="jdte:large_fuel_canister" />

Use a normal Fuel Canister, four Eclipse Alloy Ingots in four separate crafting cells, and a Time Fluid Catalyst. Existing fuel and item data are preserved. Large Pocket Generator and Large Potion Canister are not part of this Forge port yet.
