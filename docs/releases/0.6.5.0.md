# JDT Extras 0.6.5.0

Released October 21, 2026. Adds Crafting Patterns and Advanced Essence Conversion, and fixes upgrade copying, fluid bars and conversion ordering.

## Compatibility and installation

| Component | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Minecraft | 1.21.1 |
| Mod loader | NeoForge 21.1.216 or newer |
| Just Dire Things | 1.5.7 or newer |
| Java | 21 |

Install `jdte-0.6.5.0.jar` on the client and server. The optional full-grid AE2 addon is `jdte-ae-0.6.5.0.jar`; use matching JDTE and JDTE-AE versions on both sides with AE2 19.2.17 or newer in the 19.x series. The `-sources.jar` files are for development. SHA-256 checksums are included in `SHA256SUMS-0.6.5.0.txt`.

## Fixes

- **Advanced Machine Settings Copier:** applies the saved upgrade configuration exactly, reuses matching installed cards and withdraws only missing cards from the player's inventory, ordinary Upgrade Storages and Advanced Upgrade Storages, including equipped advanced cases. Surplus cards return to the player; a full inventory drops them through vanilla item return behavior. Missing cards leave the machine unchanged.
- **Fluid bars:** clamp the common JDT machine fluid bar to its display area after capacity reductions, including copied settings. Excess fluid is preserved.
- **Conversion before AE output:** both greenhouse tiers convert buffered output before each AE export. Materials waiting for enough ingredients are retained in the machine, including against pipe extraction, so raw essence can accumulate into complete crafts.

## Crafting Patterns and Advanced Essence Conversion

| Item | Registry ID | Bound patterns |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Crafting Pattern | `jdte:crafting_pattern` | Records one crafting recipe |
| Essence Conversion Upgrade | `jdte:essence_conversion_upgrade` | 1 |
| Advanced Essence Conversion Upgrade | `jdte:advanced_essence_conversion_upgrade` | 3 |

1. Craft a Pattern with eight Paper around a Crafting Table, then right-click it to open the 3×3 recipe editor.
2. Copy ingredient samples into the ghost slots. The server validates the recipe and shows its result; recording a recipe does not consume samples or produce items.
3. Place one conversion upgrade on the left and a recorded Pattern on the right of an anvil. Binding consumes the Pattern and one experience level. Duplicate, invalid and over-capacity bindings are rejected.
4. Install the upgrade in a Greenhouse or Large Greenhouse. When all recorded ingredients are available in its output buffer, it crafts the result before export.

Upgrade the ordinary card at a Smithing Table with a Netherite Upgrade Smithing Template and a Diamond. Previously bound patterns are preserved. Normal and advanced cards share the existing one-card installation limit.

Patterns match the recorded items and their components. Recipes sharing ingredients rotate by craft count to balance their outputs, and their rotation position is saved with the machine. Independent ingredient groups craft separately. Crafting remainders are preserved, output capacity is checked before consuming ingredients, and recipes are revalidated after reloads. Essences outside the configured recipes retain the original automatic conversion behavior.

Jade shows bound patterns and their validity. Item tooltips and bilingual GuideME/Patchouli pages explain editing, binding and operation. To retrieve ingredients reserved for conversion manually, remove the conversion upgrade first.

## 中文说明

### 修复

- **高级配置复制器**：按保存的配置拆装升级，复用机器已有的匹配卡，只从玩家背包、普通/高级升级存储器中补齐缺卡，支持 Curios 中的高级存储器。多余升级退还玩家，背包满时按原版方式掉落；缺少升级时不修改机器。
- **流体条越界**：容量升级减少或复制配置后，将公共 JDT 流体条限制在 GUI 范围内，保留槽中已有的超量流体。
- **转化与 AE 输出顺序**：普通和大型温室每次向 AE 输出前，先处理缓冲区中的转化。尚未凑齐配方的原料会留在机器中积累，也不会被管道提前抽走。

### 新增合成样板与高级精华转化升级

- **合成样板**（`jdte:crafting_pattern`）：用 8 张纸围绕工作台合成。右键打开 **3×3 → 1** 的样品配方编辑器，服务端检测有效性并显示产物，记录配方不消耗样品。
- **铁砧绑定**：左侧放单张精华转化升级，右侧放已记录有效配方的样板，每次消耗样板和 **1 级经验**。普通版最多绑定 **1 个样板**。
- **高级精华转化升级**（`jdte:advanced_essence_conversion_upgrade`）：最多绑定 **3 个样板**。用下界合金升级锻造模板、普通精华转化升级和钻石进行锻造，保留原先绑定的样板。两种转化卡共用每台机器 **1 张** 的安装限制。
- 安装后检测温室产物中的配方材料，凑齐后自动合成并输出，精确匹配物品及组件。共享原料的样板按合成次数**轮流执行**，轮转位置随机器存档保存；不同原料的配方分别合成。
- 合成保留返还物，消耗原料前检查输出容量，重载后重新校验配方。未被样板配方使用的精华保留原有自动转化逻辑。
- 补齐 **Jade、tooltip 和中英文游戏内说明**。需要手动取回被保留的转化原料时，先卸下转化升级。
- 主模组与配套可选 JDTE-AE 附属均更新为 **0.6.5.0**；客户端与服务端请使用匹配版本。
